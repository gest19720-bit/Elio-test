Product status

Elio is a live MVP in active development. It is provided as-is, without guarantees of uptime, specific features, or launch dates. Features may change, appear, or be removed as the product evolves — founding-group members hear about significant changes first.

Your account

  • Provide accurate information when creating an account, and keep your login credentials private.
  • You're responsible for the activity that happens in your workspace, including content you add and approvals you grant.
  • Your workspace records — customers, products, tasks, and activity — belong to you. Ask us to export or delete them at any time.

Approvals & AI actions

Elio is built so that nothing sends or changes without your approval. AI-drafted messages wait in Approvals until you approve or reject them, and every action is recorded in your activity log. When you approve an action, you're confirming you want it carried out — so review drafts before approving them.

MCP tokens

  • Tokens you create grant the scopes you choose — read-only by default — to the client you connect them to.
  • Treat tokens like passwords: they're shown once and can't be retrieved later. If one leaks, revoke it from the MCP dashboard; existing clients stop working immediately.
  • Elio stores only a SHA-256 hash of each token and logs every request made with it.

Referral program

  • Referral rewards are based on the number of people who successfully join the waitlist using your referral link.
  • Self-referrals and duplicate signups using the same email address don't count.
  • We may adjust or discontinue the referral program at any time; the terms in effect when you refer someone apply to that referral.

Acceptable use

  • Don't use Elio to send spam, harass anyone, or break the law.
  • Don't attempt to access other accounts' data, bypass row-level security, or overload the service.
  • Don't share, resell, or redistribute MCP tokens beyond the clients you authorized.

Accounts that break these rules may be suspended.

Changes

These terms will evolve as Elio moves from private build to public launch. Material changes will be communicated to account holders before they take effect.

Contact

Questions about these terms can be sent through our contact page.
