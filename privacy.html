Privacy Policy

Your data, treated with respect.

How Elio handles information from the waitlist, your workspace, and the tools you connect.

Last updated: September 2026

⚠ Plain-language draft. This policy covers the private-build MVP. Have it reviewed by counsel before relying on it at a public launch.

What we collect

There are two places Elio holds information about you:

  • The waitlist. When you join, we collect what you share in the form: name, email, business name, business type and size, the challenges you select, the tools you use, any optional notes, and the referral code that brought you (if any).
  • Your workspace. When you create an Elio account, we store the business details you enter and the records you add — customers and leads, products, tasks, approvals, workflow settings, and an activity log of actions taken by you and by Elio.

How we use it

  • To run your workspace: show your dashboard, daily brief, metrics, and records to you and only you.
  • To prepare drafts and summaries — a follow-up draft, an assistant answer, a metric insight — from your own data.
  • To tell you when Elio reaches milestones you asked to hear about, and to prioritize what gets built next.
  • To track waitlist referrals, if you were invited by another member.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use your business data to advertise to anyone.

AI features

Features like follow-up drafting, Ask Elio, and metric insights send the minimum workspace context needed to a server-side AI function in order to generate a response. Two rules apply:

  • Nothing is sent or changed without you. AI output always lands as a draft for your review — in Approvals or as a read-only answer. Elio never messages a customer or edits your records on its own.
  • Answers show their sources. When Elio answers a question, it shows which parts of your workspace the answer came from.

MCP connections

If you connect an external tool (such as an MCP client) to your workspace:

  • Access tokens are scoped and read-only by default, and you choose the permissions.
  • Elio stores only a SHA-256 hash of each token — the full token is shown once, when you create it, and cannot be read back.
  • Every request is logged (tool, timestamp, latency, result), and you can revoke a connection at any time, which stops existing clients immediately.

How we store it

Waitlist and workspace data are stored securely in Supabase with row-level security, so your records are only readable by your own account. AI features run through server-side functions, and API keys for those functions stay on the server — never in your browser.

Your choices

  • Ask us to remove your waitlist details at any time.
  • Delete workspace records yourself, or ask us to delete your account and everything in it.
  • Revoke any MCP connection with one click from the MCP dashboard.

Contact

Questions or requests about this policy can be sent through our contact page — privacy requests are handled first.
